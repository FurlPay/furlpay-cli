Conventions used everywhere:

  • Placeholders (<G...>, 0x…, <uuid>) must be replaced with your values. No real keys, seed phrases, or production credentials appear in these pages — ever.
  • Status labels (LIVE / SIMULATED / LOCAL / BLOCKED) follow the Honesty Matrix and are printed by the CLI itself alongside results.
  • Nothing here weakens a gate: recipes that move value say so, and the gates (risk engine, mandate policy, live master gate) are shown as they behave, not bypassed.
